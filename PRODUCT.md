# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React-based immersive web experience, required by the requested React Three Fiber workflow; hosting remains undecided while the first version is reviewed locally.

## Users

A married couple preserving and revisiting their shared travel memories over years and trips across the United States.

## Product Purpose

A private-feeling living atlas where the couple can explore trips by state, tell stories through varied editorial layouts, and attach voice notes to each place. Success means revisiting the site feels like stepping back into a memory rather than reading a blog archive.

## Positioning

The primary mechanism is a spatial, cinematic atlas: states are portals into distinct memory worlds, not repeated posts in one template.

## Operating Context

The couple has visited roughly 25 states and will continue adding trips. They will browse together on desktop and phones, revisit past stories, and record personal audio postcards for individual states.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive state-based exploration and trip chapters.
- Visually varied state stories within one coherent identity.
- Voice-note recording interface; durable microphone capture and storage can follow after the design prototype.
- Responsive behavior for desktop and mobile.
- This phase is a local reviewable prototype before full production and hosting decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Working name: “Wander, Together.” Voice is intimate, observant, warm, and never generic travel-influencer copy.

## Evidence on Hand

No personal trip photos, dates, names, or recordings have been supplied yet. Prototype memories and destinations must be clearly illustrative and easy to replace.

## Product Principles

- Make memory feel spatial and sensory, not chronological and administrative.
- Preserve variety between states without losing navigation consistency.
- Keep private moments emotionally central and interface chrome secondary.
- Design for two people adding to the archive over many years.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, reduced-transparency support, and usable mobile controls are required.
